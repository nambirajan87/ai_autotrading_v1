### 1. The "Momentum Decay" Profit Booker (Preventing +5% Gains from Reversing to Stops)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Problem:** In your earlier backtest, you noticed trades that surged past **+5% or +10% gain**, but because the target was higher, price reversed all the way down and hit the full Stop-Loss.

**How to use `C1...Cn`:**
* **Trigger condition:** The trade enters a profit milestone (e.g., gain $\ge +5\%$).
* **Decision Rule:**
  * **For LONG:** If the score on the current candle ($C_k$) drops to **$\le +1$** (or shows 2 consecutive falling candles like `+6` $\rightarrow$ `+3` $\rightarrow$ `0`):
    * **Action:** **Book Profit immediately** at the market price.
  * **For SHORT:** If the score rises to **$\ge -1$** (e.g., `-6` $\rightarrow$ `-2` $\rightarrow$ `+1`):
    * **Action:** **Cover & Book Profit immediately**.
* **Outcome:** You lock in a +4% to +7% gain instead of watching the trade decay back to a -2% loss.

### 2. The "Trend-Rider" (Continue Momentum Beyond Standard Targets)

> [!TIP]
> **The Problem:** Fixed target exits (e.g. taking profit at exactly 5%) often cut massive winning runners (15%–25% trends) prematurely.

**How to use `C1...Cn`:**
* **Trigger condition:** The trade reaches your initial target (e.g., +5% or +10%).
* **Decision Rule:**
  * **For LONG:** If $C_k \ge +4$ (bullish confluence remains exceptionally strong with EMA, VWAP, MACD, and Volume aligned):
    * **Action:** **Do NOT exit.** Keep the trade open and activate an aggressive trailing stop (e.g., trail below the low of the last 2 candles).
  * **For SHORT:** If $C_k \le -4$ (bearish confluence remains very strong):
    * **Action:** **Keep riding the short.**
* **Exit Trigger:** Only exit when the score finally cools down (e.g., $C_k$ drops below $+2$ for LONG or rises above $-2$ for SHORT).

### 3. The "Defensive Early Cut" (Cutting Losers at -0.5% Instead of -2.0%)

> [!WARNING]
> **The Problem:** Hard stop losses force you to absorb the full loss (e.g., -?900) even when the market thesis broke down within the first 3 candles after entry.

**How to use `C1...Cn`:**
* **Trigger condition:** Inspect the first few candles right after entry ($C_1$ to $C_3$).
* **Decision Rule (Score Inversion):**
  * **For LONG:** You entered with score `+5`. By candle $C_2$ or $C_3$, the score flips negative (e.g., `-2` or `-3`, meaning price lost VWAP and EMA crossed down).
  * **Action:** **Exit immediately with a tiny loss** (-0.3% to -0.5%).
  * **For SHORT:** You entered with score `-5`. By candle $C_2$ or $C_3$, the score flips positive (`+2` or `+3`).
  * **Action:** **Exit immediately**.
* **Outcome:** Instead of paying -?900 three times in a row (-?2,700 total), early cutoffs reduce those to -?150 to -?250 each.
